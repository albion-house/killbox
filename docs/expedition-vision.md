# Expedition Vision

## Core Direction

Expedition is the adventure mode.

It is not Arena with different scenery.

Expedition is moving toward a loop of exploration, discovery, resource management, and tactical pressure.

The intended feel is:

- Ikari Warriors energy
- Gauntlet-style adventure pressure
- Zelda-style exploration and curiosity

The overworld should feel like part of the expedition, not a lobby between fights.

---

## Overworld Goals

Overworld play should emphasize:

- Exploration
- Curiosity
- Treasure
- Weapon upgrades
- Unexpected discoveries
- Multiplayer adventure

The player should feel rewarded for poking into unknown spaces.

The player should also feel tension when they overcommit.

---

## Expedition Pacing

Expedition should breathe.

That means pressure is followed by moments that let the player explore, orient, and make choices.

The mode should alternate between:

- Exploration
- Combat waves
- Dungeons
- Relays

That alternation keeps Expedition distinct from Arena and gives the player a reason to care about route choice and room state.

---

## Expedition 01

Current Expedition 01 should be treated as a dungeon archetype:

**The Pressure Cooker**

Characteristics:

- Locked rooms
- Ambushes
- Core management
- Defensive play
- High tension

This is a strong shape, but it is not the only shape.

Future dungeon archetypes may include:

- Pressure Cooker
- Fortress
- Exploration-focused
- Defense-focused
- Boss-focused

Do not assume all future dungeons should resemble Expedition 01.

---

## Design Implications

Expedition should support:

- Defensible spaces
- Treasure discovery
- Room identities
- Route choice
- Moments of relief between spikes
- Multiplayer coordination

Expedition should avoid becoming a pure sequence of locked combat rooms with no breathing room.

---

## Success Criteria

Expedition is working when the player:

- Wants to push forward
- Wants to stop and search
- Cares where the Core goes
- Feels rewarded by discovery
- Feels tension from risk
- Remembers specific rooms
