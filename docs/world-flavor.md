# WORLD FLAVOR

## Core Feeling

Killbox should feel strange, curious, and slightly impossible.

The player should often ask:

> What is that thing?

before asking:

> How does that thing work?

Mystery comes before explanation.

Wonder comes before lore.

---

## Influences

Primary Influence:

- Buckaroo Banzai

Secondary Influences:

- Blade Runner
- Subtle Lovecraft
- Small amounts of Warhammer 40K scale

These are flavor influences, not lore templates.

---

## We Favor

- Strange artifacts
- Lost technology
- Alternate realities
- Impossible machinery
- Forgotten experiments
- Dimensional anomalies
- Weird creatures
- Objects with stories attached to them
- Things that feel discovered rather than invented

---

## We Avoid

- Generic fantasy kingdoms
- Ancient prophecies
- Chosen ones
- Elves
- Dwarves
- Orcs
- Magic crystals
- Generic RPG factions
- Lore that explains everything

---

## Item Philosophy

Items should feel like discoveries.

Bad:

> Forged by the Frost Kingdom.

Good:

> 1983 Japanese prototype recovered from a collapsed timeline.

Bad:

> An ancient magical staff.

Good:

> Found in a maintenance tunnel that should not exist.

---

## Creature Philosophy

Creatures should be memorable silhouettes first.

Favor:

- Biomechanical hybrids
- Emotional monsters
- Multi-eyed creatures
- Strange anatomy
- Handmade weirdness

Avoid:

- Generic zombies
- Generic demons
- Generic fantasy monsters

---

## The Albion House Test

When creating new content ask:

- Is this weird enough to be interesting?
- Does it create curiosity?
- Does it feel discovered?
- Would players tell a story about finding it?
